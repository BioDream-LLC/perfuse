CMS-0057-F

The four APIs the rule requires

What it is

CMS-0057-F, the Interoperability and Prior Authorization rule, tells payers to share data with patients, doctors and other plans over FHIR, and to make prior authorization electronic.

Why it exists

Prior authorization by fax and phone delays care. Patients changing plans lose their history. The rule sets deadlines to fix both.

How you use it

Start Perfuse with its FHIR server on, load your members and claims through channels, and open the CMS-0057 screen: it says which APIs are ready and what each still needs.

Electronic prior authorization

From the doctor's order to the decision, without a fax

Three Da Vinci guides work in sequence. CRD tells the doctor, while ordering, whether approval is needed. DTR gathers the documentation with a questionnaire filled from the record. PAS sends the request and returns the decision.

  • A coverage rules file the payer writes in plain YAML, answered over CDS Hooks
  • A reviewer's queue in the console: read the request and its documents, then approve, deny or approve fewer units
  • Pended decisions sent back by subscription the moment they are made
  • Requests passed on to your utilization management system as X12 278s, over CAQH CORE SOAP or MIME, SFTP batch files or HTTPS, and its answers (and its 999 and TA1 receipts) brought back; set up and watched in the console
  • Both releases EHRs use: CRD 2.2.1, DTR 2.2.0 and PAS 2.2.1, and the 2.1.0 of each that CMS-0057 points EHRs at, answered in the release the request names (or the one the client is registered for). Every version
  • A provider's $inquire also asks your UM system, so it finds authorizations requested by fax, portal or phone
  • Inferno: CRD 92 pass and none failed, PAS 82 of 84, DTR 43 pass and 2 fail; 2.1.0 by each guide's own examples, with no validator errors
  • A decision service hook: send each request to your own engine, or to Vaglio (source-available), which explains every decision; Perfuse never requires one

Provider directory

Who is in network, where, and for what

What it is

Da Vinci Plan-Net is the FHIR form of a plan's provider directory: practitioners, their roles, locations, networks, services and insurance plans.

Why it exists

CMS requires plans to publish their directory as an open API, so apps can tell members which doctors near them take their plan.

How you use it

Load the directory through a channel from your credentialing system, and apps search it on the built-in FHIR server.

  • Every Plan-Net search, include and reverse chain, for example "practitioners who work at this location"
  • Inferno Plan-Net 1.1.0: 199 pass; the 10 left are the test kit choosing its search values (details)

Quick start

A prior authorization server in three steps

1. Write the coverage rules: which codes need approval, and what documentation. 2. Start Perfuse with its FHIR server, CRD and PAS on. 3. Point an EHR, or Inferno, at it, and open Payer, CMS-0057 in the console.

To pass requests on to the review system you already run, add its address and its connectivity method: the console's Settings, Payers, Utilization management link has every field.

The CMS-0057 chapter of the manual 
# payer-rules.yaml
payer: Springfield Health Plan
rules:
  - description: Home oxygen equipment
    codes: [E0424, E0431]
    covered: covered
    pa: auth-needed
    documentation: [clinical]

# start it
perfuse serve -fhir -pas -crd-rules payer-rules.yaml

# optional: decide in your UM system, over CAQH CORE SOAP
UM_PASSWORD=... perfuse serve -fhir -pas -crd-rules payer-rules.yaml \
  -pas-278-transport core-soap -pas-278-url https://um.example/CORE \
  -pas-278-receiver ACMEUM -pas-278-user perfuse \
  -pas-278-password-env UM_PASSWORD
