The UK and Europe

A free HL7 and FHIR integration engine for European hospitals

Perfuse connects the hospital's systems (patient administration, laboratory, radiology, PACS, document registries) over HL7 v2, FHIR, DICOM and IHE. It is free under Apache 2.0, runs on your own servers so the data never leaves them, and takes over your Mirth Connect channels.

Why now

Mirth Connect is no longer free. Your interfaces still have to run.

From version 4.6 (March 2025) Mirth Connect is sold under a commercial licence. Many European hospitals run the last open-source release, which no longer gets fixes, or one of its forks. Perfuse is a different engine, not a fork, and it was built to make leaving easy and safe.

ICON:swap

Your channels come with you

Perfuse reads channel exports from Mirth 4.5.2, the Open Integration Engine and BridgeLink, runs their JavaScript unchanged, and can export back: each of those servers accepts the channel files Perfuse writes.

ICON:eye

Proved on your own traffic

Shadow mode runs a channel beside the live engine on real messages and compares every output, before anything is switched over.

ICON:server

One file, no Java

One program for Linux, Windows or macOS, on Intel or Arm. It holds 31 MB of memory at rest, against Mirth's 383 MB on the same machine.

ICON:key

Free, and stays free

Apache 2.0: use it, change it, run it for others. No licence fee and no paid edition.

Data that stays put

It runs inside your network. If you host it in the cloud, -residency eea (or uk, ch) refuses any connector outside the region you allow.

ICON:shield

GDPR and UK GDPR

Find everything held about a person in every store, give them a copy, and erase it with a second administrator's approval. Legal holds are respected, and the record of processing (Article 30) is produced from the channels.

ICON:users

37 national patient numbers

The NHS number, CHI, KVNR, BSN, the French INS, codice fiscale, the Nordic personal numbers and more. Each is checked by its check digit, so a mistyped number never links two people.

ICON:book

National code systems

SNOMED CT national editions in the country's language, ICD-10-GM and OPS, CIM-10 FR, the UK's ICD-10 and OPCS-4, WHO ICD-10 and ICD-11.

ICON:doc

IHE document sharing

An XDS.b registry and repository, an XCA gateway, MHD, PIXm, PDQm and XDM, tested against the NIST XDS Toolkit. Plus the International Patient Summary.

ICON:globe

Twelve languages

The console and the manual in English, German, French, Dutch, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Polish, Swedish, Danish, Norwegian and Finnish.

Tested against national profiles

UK Core, FR Core and ISiK, with the results published

Perfuse turned national sample HL7 v2 messages into FHIR, and the official HL7 validator checked every resource against each country's profiles. Each failure has a reason, and the reasons are listed.

What is not done yet

  • No hospital runs Perfuse in production yet. If you want to be the first, we would like to hear from you, and shadow mode lets you try it on your own traffic without risk.
  • The translations were made by machine and have not been reviewed by native speakers.
  • Not certified anywhere: no gematik confirmation for ISiK, no national agency approval. The results above are our own runs of the official tools, published so anyone can repeat them.
  • The hosting templates for Frankfurt, Ireland and London have been planned against a real AWS account but never applied. The draft data processing agreement has not been read by a lawyer.
  • Support comes from the BioDream team, through GitHub.
